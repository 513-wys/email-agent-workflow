The problem

Important context is split across the inbox.

Search finds messages. It does not reliably explain what changed, what still needs action, or which email supports the answer.

01

One topic, many messages

A new message can revise an earlier deadline or decision. Reading one email is not enough.

02

Forwarding hides identity

Mail forwarded from university or work accounts can appear to come from the mailbox owner.

03

Noise looks actionable

Subscriptions, confirmations and real tasks compete for the same attention.

The product

One workflow from inbox to evidence.

Deterministic checks protect the boundary. AI structures the content. Local retrieval keeps cross-email answers grounded.

The advantage

Useful because it stays inspectable.

Every result remains connected to its origin—and the evaluation reports both strengths and failures.

Design principle

Evidence before confidence.

Bilingual output, source citations, safe synthetic demo data and no autonomous mailbox actions.
